# T022 — Escape-Cost Nonidentifiability from Current ECD Structure

## Status
**COMPLETED — MATHEMATICAL SEPARATION, NOT YET FULL NOVELTY**

Run 35354518854 succeeded.

Paired systems have identical worlds, decisions, and outcomes under the current experiment language. Thus their current ECD/observational structure is identical and decision resolution is impossible in both. Their external synthesis costs are 1 versus m.

Tested m = 2,...,1024. The escape-cost ratio grows from 2 to 1024, with identical current ECD structure throughout.

## Candidate theorem
Current observational/ECD structure alone does not determine the minimum cost of escaping observational equivalence. The ratio of escape costs between systems with identical current ECD structure can be arbitrarily large.

## Limitation / novelty boundary
This does NOT establish irreducibility against a model that is given the full synthesis/design space and its costs. Once that information is included, general OED/design optimization can distinguish the systems. The next test must make synthesis cost/availability itself latent under current evidence and ask whether the cheapest resolving experiment is identifiable before additional meta-evidence is acquired.
